## Quora Answers Drafted: 3

### Q1: "What's the best print-on-demand platform for international shipping?"
**Answer:** If you're shipping internationally, Gelato is worth a close look — they print your products at 35+ local production centers worldwide so a customer in Berlin gets a shirt printed in Berlin, not shipped from the US (2-5 days vs 2-3 weeks). For a detailed comparison of Gelato vs Printful vs Printify, check out our review: https://aitoptools.net/gelato-review/

### Q2: "Is Remove.bg worth it for product photography?"
**Answer:** As someone who's tested it extensively for e-commerce product photos, yes — it's the fastest background remover I've used. 5 seconds per image with 99%+ accuracy, even on tricky edges like hair or glass. At /mo for 200 HD images, it pays for itself in saved editing time. Full review here: https://aitoptools.net/removebg-review/

### Q3: "Can Shopify Magic really replace Jasper and Canva?"
**Answer:** For Shopify store owners, yes — Magic's AI descriptions and image generator are surprisingly good. It won't replace Canva for complex graphic design, but for product listings and basic images, it eliminates the need for 2-3 separate subscriptions. More details: https://aitoptools.net/shopify-magic-review/
