If you sell on Amazon, Etsy, or Shopify, you've had a listing rejected for a policy violation. You fix it. You re-submit. It gets flagged again for a different reason. The cycle takes hours per listing, and a single policy mistake can get your account suspended.

GreenOnion's pitch is simple: a 60-second flow that takes a product photo and outputs listing copy that is, by construction, compliant with the platform's published rules. No trial-and-error, no manual policy lookup, no "is this keyword allowed here" guessing. Just feed it a photo, wait 60 seconds, and you have a listing that's ready to submit.

We tested GreenOnion for 14 days across 30 test listings (10 Amazon, 10 Etsy, 10 Shopify — overlap allowed) to see if the 60-second claim holds, and whether the compliance-first approach beats the scoring-first approach of tools like Picjam.

## Use Cases: When to Pick GreenOnion

Based on our test, GreenOnion is the better choice for:
- **New sellers on Amazon** who don't know all the policy rules yet
- **Multi-platform sellers** who need consistent compliance across 3-4 channels
- **Bulk listing** scenarios where you have 100+ products to publish quickly
- **Account safety focus** sellers who've been suspended before and need bulletproof listings

GreenOnion is probably NOT the best choice for:
- **Conversion optimization focus** (use Picjam instead)
- **A+ Content generation** (neither does this well; you need Jasper or a designer)
- **Brand voice consistency** (GreenOnion's outputs feel more generic than Picjam's)
