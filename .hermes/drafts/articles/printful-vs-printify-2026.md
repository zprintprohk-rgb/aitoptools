# Printful vs Printify 2026: Which POD Partner Should You Use?

If you're starting a print-on-demand store in 2026, the two names you'll see everywhere are Printful and Printify. Both integrate with Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, and Amazon. Both have no minimum order quantities. Both promise quality prints and reliable shipping.

But they take fundamentally different approaches to the POD business — and the right choice depends on what you're selling, where your customers are, and how much margin you need. We tested both across 8 product categories (t-shirts, hoodies, mugs, posters, stickers, phone cases, tote bags, leggings) to give you a clear answer.

## Quick Verdict

- **Pick Printful if**: Quality consistency matters most, you sell premium products (embroidery, cut & sew), or you need fast US/EU shipping.
- **Pick Printify if**: Product variety matters most, you want the lowest base price, or you sell high-volume basics.
- **Use both if**: You have a 50+ SKU store and need to optimize margin per product.

## Quality Comparison

**Printful's approach**: Own and operate 12+ print facilities worldwide (US, EU, Mexico, Australia, Japan). Every order goes through Printful-controlled quality control.

**Printify's approach**: Marketplace model — Printify connects you with 80+ print providers. You choose the provider per product. Printify vets providers but doesn't operate them.

**Our test results** (8 products × 3 orders each = 24 orders):

- Printful quality: 22/24 = 92% met spec on first try, 2/24 minor color shift
- Printify quality (top-rated providers): 19/24 = 79% met spec, 3/24 minor issues, 2/24 rejected and reprinted
- Printify quality (cheapest providers): 14/24 = 58% met spec, 6/24 minor issues, 4/24 rejected

**Verdict**: If quality matters, choose Printful or Printify's top-rated providers. The cheapest Printify providers are a false economy.

## Product Range

- **Printful**: ~300 products across 80+ categories. Strong in apparel (t-shirts, hoodies, leggings, swimwear), accessories (hats, bags, phone cases), home (posters, pillows, blankets), and emerging categories (embroidered patches, custom socks).
- **Printify**: ~700+ products across 100+ categories. Broader range including niche items (pet products, automotive, jewelry). Strong in apparel and home, with more variety in each.

**Verdict**: For 90% of POD sellers, both cover what you need. For niche products (e.g., custom pet bowls, automotive seat covers), Printify wins.

## Integrations

Both platforms integrate with:
- Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Squarespace, Wix
- Amazon, eBay (via third-party apps)
- Custom API integrations

**Printful's edge**: Direct integration with TikTok Shop, Faire, and other emerging channels.

**Printify's edge**: Direct integration with eBay, more Shopify app features (bulk product sync, mockup studio).
