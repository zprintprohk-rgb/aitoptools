# Capterra Review Draft — Placeit

## Section 1: Overall Rating (4.5/5)

**Headline**: "Best mockup library for POD sellers — 50,000+ templates and AI background removal saved me hundreds of hours"

**Summary** (150 words max):
I've been using Placeit for 18 months for my Shopify POD store with 200+ products. The mockup library is unmatched — 50,000+ t-shirt mockups across every brand, cut, and color. The AI background removal is good enough that I skip Photoshop for 90% of mockups. The yearly plan ($89.46) is more cost-effective than monthly Photoshop + stock mockup subscriptions.

Pros: Massive mockup library, AI background removal, AI design upscaler, direct mockup downloads, commercial use
Cons: Yearly plan only, no native POD fulfillment, smaller AI design library than Kittl, design library could be better organized

## Section 2: Pros

- **50,000+ t-shirt mockups**: Gildan, Bella+Canvas, Next Level, American Apparel, every brand. Every cut (unisex, women's, fitted, youth), every color.
- **AI background removal**: Built-in, fast, accurate. Replaces Photoshop for 90% of mockup work.
- **Mockup batch generation**: Generate 20+ mockups for a new product line in under 1 hour.
- **AI design upscaler**: Take a low-res design and upscale to print-ready resolution.
- **Direct PNG download**: 2000×2000+ PNG files ready for Shopify product photos.
- **Commercial use on all paid plans**: Use mockups as product photos, social media, marketing.
- **100,000+ design library**: Pre-made graphics to add to your mockups.

## Section 3: Cons

- **Yearly plan only**: $89.46/yr (~$7.46/mo billed annually). No monthly option. High upfront cost.
- **Single category subscriptions**: $47.46/yr per category (t-shirts, hoodies, mugs, etc.). For 5+ categories, you need the full $89.46/yr.
- **No native POD fulfillment**: Like Kittl, Placeit is design + mockup only. You still need Printful/Printify.
- **AI design generation is secondary**: Placeit's AI features are background removal + upscaling, not full design generation. Kittl is better for AI design.
- **Design library organization**: 100,000+ designs can be hard to find. Search function is decent but not great.
- **Limited vector output**: Most downloads are PNG/JPG. Vector (SVG) not standard.
- **No mobile app**: Web-only. Mockup generation requires desktop browser.

## Section 4: Reviewer-Specific Details

**Use case**: T-shirt, hoodie, and mug mockups for Shopify POD store

**How long used**: 18 months

**Team size**: Solo seller + 1 part-time VA

**Specific examples**:
- T-shirt mockups: Excellent (every brand, every color)

- Mug mockups: Good (limited to standard 11oz and 15oz)
- Phone case mockups: Good (covers iPhone 6 to 15)
- Poster mockups: Good (limited frame options)
- Hat mockups: Limited (only baseball caps, no beanies or dad hats)

**Time saved per product**:
- Before Placeit: 30-60 minutes per mockup (Photoshop + stock photos)
- With Placeit: 2-5 minutes per mockup
- Time saved: ~30 minutes per product × 200 products = 100 hours per year

**ROI calculation**:
- Cost: $89.46/yr
- Time saved: 100 hours/yr × $20/hr (my time value) = $2,000/yr
- Net benefit: $1,910/yr

**Alternatives considered**:
- Photoshop: More flexible but 10x slower
- Smartmockups: Smaller library, no AI features
- Mockey: Good free option, but smaller library and AI design limitations
- DIY mockups: Free but extremely time-consuming

**Why I switched from Photoshop**: Time savings. Placeit paid for itself in the first month.

## Section 5: Bottom Line

Placeit is the best mockup library for POD sellers in 2026. If you're placing 5+ products per month, the $89.46/yr will pay for itself within the first month. Skip if you're a hobby seller with 1-2 products per month — Mockey's free tier is enough.
