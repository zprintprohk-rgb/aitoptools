## NOT Friendly for Print Sellers

Photoroom isn't perfect for every POD scenario. Here's where it falls short:

### ❌ Complex Product Mockups (Multi-Design)
If you need a mockup showing **front + back + close-up** of the same design in one image, Photoroom's templates are limited. You'll need to composite multiple exports in Canva or Photoshop.

### ❌ Fabric Texture Accuracy
AI mockups approximate how a design looks on fabric — they don't show actual print texture (DTG screen-printed feel, puff print texture, glitter effect). For customers who need to see the actual fabric print quality, Photoroom's mockups can be misleading.

### ❌ Custom Mockups (Real Product Photography Shots)
Photoroom's template library covers the 20 most common POD products. If you sell **unusual items** (yoga mats, blankets, pet accessories), you may not find a matching mockup template. You'd need to create custom mockups via photography or 3D rendering tools.

### ❌ Export Resolution Limits
On the Free plan, exports top out at 1080px. Even the Pro plan exports max out at 4K. For large-format print catalogs (posters, banners) that need ultra-high-res proofs, you'll want a dedicated tool.

### ❌ No Video Product Demos
Photoroom is photo-only. If you need short product videos for TikTok Shop or Amazon listing videos, you'll need a separate tool like Canva Video or Runway ML.

### ❌ No Direct Etsy/Amazon Integration
You still need to download and upload photos to each platform manually. Some competitors offer direct marketplace integrations.
